# About "Above the Ruin"

**Above the Ruin** is a cozy game, such as Stardew Valley or Harvest Moon.
However, I've decided to go with darker tones and themes.

The game is set in a post-apocalyptic setting, where survivors live on the rooftops of skyscrapers.

The rooftop is your home. Here, you'll be able to do all the cozy things, such as farming, tending to your animals, and so on.

If you fancy leaving the safety of the rooftops, you can choose to descend through the tower. 
Here, you'll be able to fight off enemies, explore abandoned homes and offices and find loot to improve life on the surface.

Beyond the floors of your tower is the street level. This is the harshest area and will test your skills. 
But risk comes with reward. You'll be able to find very rare items down there, and items that can be used
to improve surface life, such as car batteries.

You aren't alone in your struggle for survival. As the game progresses, you'll discover other towers with survivors. 
You'll be able to connect towers with bridges and other methods in order to meet other communities, for better or worse.

**Above the Ruin** is an RPG at heart, and there is a narrative behind it all, that I won't spoil here.
