Following on from [Pixel Art is Hard](/posts/pixel-art-is-hard), I decided to spend some more time creating props for **Above the Ruin**.

One of the important aspects of the game will be water. There will be a few ways of obtaining it, from basic buckets to water collection systems.
For now, I spent some time creating a tank, which will collect a small amount of water passively. This can be upgraded with a tarp to funnel
more water in to the tank, increasing the generation.

Then there's the fire pits, lit and unlit. These can also be upgraded to add a metal rack, meaning you can cook more at once.

Finally, there's a crucible. This will serve as the main source of metal. You'll be able to collect scrap metal from the floors of the tower, 
and melt them in the crucible, turning them in to ingots that you can use.

![Prop Examples](/images/prop-examples.png)

As always, there's improvements that can be made and I'll probably revisit these at some point. But I'm happy with how they've turned out so 
far, and I've loved doing it.
