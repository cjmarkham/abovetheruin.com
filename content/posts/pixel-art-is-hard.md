I'm at the stage now where I have a few systems in place, but I'm tired of staring at basic shapes
and a grey-boxed world.

I am a backend engineer, so stuff like art doesn't come naturally to me. However, I've always admired
pixel art, having grown up in the age of Mario on the Snes. I was also inspired by Eric Barone, the 
creator of Stardew Valley. He managed to do all of the pixel art himself.

So I'm taking a plunge in to the world of pixel art, learning things such as pillow shading (and why it's not that good),
and avoiding double pixels.

I've decided to focus on one item at a time, and chosen a chicken coop to start with. I want **Above the Ruin** to have a
"ruined" asthetic, but I also want it to be colourful in places. My inspiration for this is Dying Light, where the buildings are all
wood and grey/brown, but colour is added through things like tarp roofs/walls or sheet metal.

So my first design of the chicken coop tried to replicate exactly that.

![chicken coop](/images/chicken-coop-1.png)

This was alright, but the lines felt too harsh for me. I also wanted to try different colours for the wood, and try different
shades of blue for the sheet metal. All of this is to say that one item cn go through many variations while experimenting, as you can see here:

![chicken coops](/images/chicken-coops.png)

The final design is a variation on all of those, with a darker wood and lighter roof, with soft lines.

![chicken coop final](/images/chicken-coop-final.png)

I still want to play around with this, and things might change once I get a few more items done and it's placed in to the game.
However, I'm pleased with the result so far.
